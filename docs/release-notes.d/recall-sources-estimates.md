The tracker now reads layoff announcements from the main press wires, and when a company's SEC filing gives only a percentage of its workforce, it shows an estimated headcount. Every estimate is labelled as one and shows the arithmetic (the percentage times the employee count from the company's latest annual report) next to the filing's own words.
