A company's shrinking workforce, or the combined net job change of a whole group of companies, is no longer counted as one layoff. Two such figures (Wells Fargo 10,000 and the DAX companies' 46,000) were removed on 9 November and disclosed in the corrections log; the tracker now refuses that kind of number at the point it is read.
