Layoff news now leads every edition. Blog reads sit in a labelled "Career resources" section, only for readers who ticked the articles box, and never make up a whole edition for a layoff-list reader. The career subject line leads with the reader benefit ("3 ways to strengthen your applications") and no longer carries article titles that name other products. Each read has a "Why it matters" line and a clear call to action ("Read the guide", "Read the comparison"), excerpts that are questions are replaced by the post's first real sentence, and the footer is shorter while keeping the unsubscribe link, the preferences link and the tracking disclosure.
