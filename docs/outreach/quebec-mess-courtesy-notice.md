(https://asktherecruiter.com/blog/ai-layoff-tracker/). Chaque donnée publiée

très faible volume. Si des conditions d'utilisation particulières

Hello,

I operate a free public tracker of verified layoffs
(https://asktherecruiter.com/blog/ai-layoff-tracker/); every published figure
links to its official source. We would like to present the Ministry's monthly

low volume. If specific reuse terms apply to these lists, please let us know
and we will comply.
