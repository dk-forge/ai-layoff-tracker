## Method and honest limits
- List built 2026-11-23 from the previous list's in-window events, a WARN weekly roundup
  (jobadvisor.link, 2026-11-16..22), and news searches. Search returned mixed years and
  aggregator counts, so (S) marks a count not traced to a primary source. The list is a
  proxy, not a certified ground truth. Events dated before 2026-10-24 were dropped
  (Barclays 10-22, Amex 10-23, NAB, Flutter, eBay).
- Not independently verified from a primary page: Olympus (Becker's ASC, page blocked
  403 here; the 2,000 figure comes from a search snippet), Dole, Westin Seattle, Enphase
  (WARN or 8-K via the roundup; the live rows themselves carry the filing).
- Time to first listing is the gap from the event date to the row's `created_at`. Rows
  from ERM (Eurofound) carry no `created_at`.

## Prefilter gap found and fixed in this PR
Three real 2026-11-17 Renault headlines ("...while cutting 1,000 others by 2028",
"...1,000 voluntary departures: report") are dropped by the free title prefilter in the
US edition (`no-layoff-term`). Fix: keep "cutting N others" and "voluntary
departures/redundancies/exits/buyouts". This closes a headline-shape gap, not the whole
Renault miss: other Renault headlines passed and still produced no row.
