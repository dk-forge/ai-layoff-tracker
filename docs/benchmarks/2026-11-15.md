## Method and honest limits
- List built 2026-11-15 from LayoffHedge October and November tables plus WARN
  aggregators and news search. Search returned mixed years, so most counts rest on an
  aggregator, not the company filing. Items marked (S) were not traced to a primary
  source. Treat the list as a reasonable proxy, not a certified ground truth.
- Overlap with last week's list is deliberate where the event falls inside the new
  30-day window (Barclays, Amex, Volvo, NAB, Flutter, eBay, ...).
- Excluded: Upwork (37%, no count), Ecco-style multi-country totals are kept; KLM
  (about 1,000 net loss, stored 5,000, see precision note); Royal Mail, Kroger, Netflix,
  GSK, Telia (before 10-16).
- **Time to first listing is now measurable**: `/query` exposes `created_at`. Caveat:
  rows with `created_at` of 2026-11-05 19:xx and 2026-11-11 11:39 are clusters, which fits
  the 2.20.306 insert outage (TECHLOG 2026-11-11) followed by backfill, so those lags
  include an outage and are not steady state. WARN/ERM rows can lack `created_at`.
