# Independent tracker benchmark — 2026-09-12

This is an evidence snapshot, not a ranking. Figures are copied from each
public tracker on 2026-09-12 and are not normalized across definitions.

## United States

| Tracker | Public claim observed | Scope / caveat |
|---|---|---|
| AskTheRecruiter | 38 of 56 US jurisdictions currently have a WARN collector; SEC Item 2.05 measured 100% of 45 sampled events | Also combines WARN, SEC, news and international sources; full US recall is not established |
| WARN Act Tracker | 133,208 affected workers and 1,312 validated 2026 filings | Says all 50 states and DC; official agency links remain the authority |
| WARNScan | Claims all 50 states, daily updates, and 64,127 total notices | WARN-only; public page does not expose the same verification and scope split |
| LayoffAlert | 3,476 notices, 302,402 workers, 44 states | WARN-only and explicitly says it is a subset of total layoffs |

The US comparison does not support a top-three ranking yet. Competitors have
broader public WARN-state claims, while AskTheRecruiter has a broader evidence
taxonomy. A fair comparison requires the same date basis, amendments, state
coverage, and inclusion rules.

## Europe and global

EuroLayoffs publicly describes itself as Europe-first, reports 315 tracked
events and 12,175 confirmed European cuts on its landing page, separates
company-announced from press-only figures, and links every row to a primary
source. Its global RSS panel is context, not a complete global event register.

AskTheRecruiter has 79 represented countries classified against their own
disclosure regimes, but only the US slice currently supports the word
“recall.” UK, Estonia and Taiwan are measured as shares of official national
totals, with substantial definitional mismatch; those are not comparable event
recall percentages.

## Decision

The benchmark is now refreshed enough to identify the real competitive bar, but
it does not establish a top-three position. The defensible product advantage is
source-linked cross-source aggregation with explicit uncertainty; the largest
remaining competitive gaps are verified 50-state WARN coverage, measured
regional recall, and a clean seven-run/fourteen-day GDELT reliability record.
