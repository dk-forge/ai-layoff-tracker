AI attribution watch

Employers explicitly named AI on 383 verified cuts this week. So far in 2026, 77,551 verified cuts (8%) carry an explicit employer attribution.

Zero explicit attribution does not mean AI had no effect: it means no employer in these records cited AI as a reason. We measure employer disclosure, not the effect of automation on employment.

What it means

For recruiters and job seekers: 80% of the verified cuts in the week were in Technology and Finance & Insurance, so that is where the most newly affected candidates are; in the US, the most were in Washington (3,762).
