AI attribution watch

Employers explicitly named AI on 61,700 verified cuts this week. So far in 2026, 136,721 verified cuts (16%) carry an explicit employer attribution.

Zero explicit attribution does not mean AI had no effect: it means no employer in these records cited AI as a reason. We measure employer disclosure, not the effect of automation on employment.

What it means

For recruiters and job seekers: 74% of this week's verified cuts were in Technology and Automotive, so that is where the most newly affected candidates are; in the US, the most were in California (5,715).
